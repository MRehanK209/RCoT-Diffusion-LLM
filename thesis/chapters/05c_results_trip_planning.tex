\subsection{RQ1 and RQ2: Trip Planning as a natural-language planning check}
RQ1 and RQ2 should not be answered from arithmetic planning alone. Trip Planning therefore tests whether the Countdown crossover persists under a stricter natural-language parser. The answer is yes in weaker but still meaningful form: Dream remains strongest among the standard diffusion runs at low $k$, while Llama benefits more from search and the flat-prompt Llama run becomes the strongest high-$k$ curve in the merged comparison of available runs.
