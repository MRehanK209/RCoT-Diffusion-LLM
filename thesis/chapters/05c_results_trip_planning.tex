\subsection{Trip Planning: a natural-language planning extension}
\label{sec:results-trip}

Trip Planning extends the comparison from symbolic arithmetic planning to structured natural-language planning. These results are less clean than Countdown because many outputs are rejected by the strict parser before semantic scoring. The benchmark therefore measures both planning quality and the ability to produce the expected itinerary structure. Table~\ref{tab:trip-parseability-semantic} separates parseability from semantic correctness. Within that caveat, Dream remains strongest among the standard diffusion runs at low $k$, while Llama benefits more from search and the flat-prompt Llama run becomes the strongest observed high-$k$ curve in the merged comparison. Deterministically, Dream occupies the top of the configuration ranking at $0.135$--$0.150$, with the next non-Dream model being LLaDA-Base at $0.115$ and the strongest AR configuration being Llama-Instruct flat at $0.095$; selected deterministic configurations across backends and block sizes are reproduced in Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-trip-deterministic}.
