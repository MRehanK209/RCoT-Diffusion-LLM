\subsection{Trip Planning: a natural-language planning extension}
\label{sec:results-trip}

Trip Planning extends the comparison from symbolic arithmetic planning to structured natural-language planning. Results must be interpreted together with parser failure rates and formatting sensitivity, because a natural-language itinerary can be semantically close but rejected by the strict parser. Within that caveat, Dream remains strongest among the standard diffusion runs at low $k$, while Llama benefits more from search and the flat-prompt Llama run becomes the strongest high-$k$ curve in the merged comparison. Deterministically, Dream variants occupy the top of the configuration ranking at $0.135$--$0.150$, with the next non-Dream model being LLaDA-Base at $0.115$ and the strongest AR configuration being Llama-Instruct flat at $0.095$; selected deterministic configurations across backends and block sizes are reproduced in Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-trip-deterministic}.

Dream-Instruct and Dream-Base are the strongest standard diffusion curves at low $k$, while Llama-Instruct flat reaches $0.295$ at pass@64. The Trip Planning result is not identical to Countdown because the flat prompt changes Llama substantially, but the observed ordering again differs between low-budget and many-sample evaluation.
