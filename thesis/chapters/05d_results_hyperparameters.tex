\subsection{RQ1 control: hyperparameter analysis before paradigm comparison}
Before the thesis pivoted from tuning to paradigm comparison, the repository ran a GSM8K sweep over generation length, diffusion steps, and block length for LLaDA and Dream, plus generation-length sweeps for Qwen. The sweep confirms that tuning matters, but it does not erase the paradigm-level pass@k question because even well-tuned diffusion runs and autoregressive runs change rank under different sampling budgets.

Generation length is the most important practical parameter in the sweep: 256-token generations dominate the strongest configurations. Diffusion step count has diminishing returns, and block length interacts with both quality and latency. The main thesis therefore does not attempt to declare a universally best diffusion hyperparameter setting; it asks how the paradigms compare under controlled, documented configurations.
