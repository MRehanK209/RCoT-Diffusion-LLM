\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across the three benchmarks, the comparison between diffusion and
autoregressive runs does not support a universal ranking between the two model
families. The preferred model depends on the sampling budget and on the
benchmark interface.

Countdown-cd4 gives the clearest example. LLaDA-Base and Dream-Base are
strongest at estimated stochastic pass@1, but Qwen-Base and Llama-Base overtake
them at high pass@k. Trip Planning shows a related but less clean pattern
because parser compatibility and prompt format have a stronger effect. GSM8K is
close to saturated at high $k$, making it more useful here as a diagnostic for
prompt format and answer extraction than as a paradigm-ranking benchmark.

The diffusion runs do not show the large-$k$ coverage advantage that motivated
the study. Their strength appears mainly in deterministic or low-budget planning
settings. Qwen and Llama, by contrast, often gain more from repeated stochastic
sampling when many candidates are available. The per-question analyses show
overlap, but not identity, in the problems solved by each model family. That
makes hybrid evaluation and selection methods a natural next step.

Future work should therefore move away from declaring one generation interface
superior in general. A more useful direction is to combine candidate generation
with verification, routing, selection, or parser-aware repair.
