\section{Conclusion}
This thesis draft answers the motivating question conditionally. Diffusion LLMs do not simply replace autoregressive LLMs for reasoning and planning. They change the evaluation trade-off. In deterministic and low-$k$ settings, LLaDA and Dream often produce stronger planning behavior. Under large-$k$ sampling, Qwen2.5 and Llama 3.1 can overtake because autoregressive sampling explores more diverse candidates. The strongest future direction is therefore not a simple paradigm replacement, but hybrid or ensemble evaluation that uses the complementary strengths of both generation mechanisms.
