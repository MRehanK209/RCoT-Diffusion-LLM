\subsection{RQ1 and RQ2: Countdown-cd4 ranking reversal under pass@k}
RQ1 asks whether diffusion models are better in low-sample planning, and RQ2 asks whether larger sampling budgets reverse that answer. Countdown-cd4 provides the clearest evidence that they do: diffusion models lead at pass@1, but autoregressive models overtake decisively by pass@128. Figure~\ref{fig:countdown-base} and Table~\ref{tab:countdown-base-passk} are the core empirical answer to the thesis question.

This reversal is not a small fluctuation: Qwen rises from 0.040433 to 0.513105, while LLaDA rises from 0.142854 to only 0.272177. The result supports the concentration-versus-diversity hypothesis.
