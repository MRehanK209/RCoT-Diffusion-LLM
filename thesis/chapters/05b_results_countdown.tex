\subsection{Countdown-cd4: budget-dependent ranking reversal}
\label{sec:results-countdown}

Countdown-cd4 gives the clearest ranking change across $k$. Under low-budget evaluation, the evaluated diffusion configurations are competitive or leading. As $k$ increases, some evaluated Qwen/Llama configurations gain more from repeated sampling and overtake at high pass@k. Figure~\ref{fig:countdown-base} reports the base pass@k curves.

\begin{figure}[!htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.92\textwidth]{images/countdown_base_crossover.pdf}
\caption{Countdown-cd4 base pass@k crossover. The evaluated diffusion base configurations lead at estimated stochastic pass@1; Qwen-Base and Llama-Base overtake by large $k$. Paired bootstrap tests over questions find LLaDA-Base $>$ Qwen-Base at $k=1$ and Qwen-Base $>$ LLaDA-Base at $k=128$ (Table~\ref{tab:paired-bootstrap-tests}).}
\label{fig:countdown-base}
\end{figure}

The reversal is visible in the rankings: at estimated stochastic pass@1 the ordering is LLaDA-Base $>$ Dream-Base $>$ Qwen-Base $>$ Llama-Base. At pass@128 it becomes Qwen-Base $>$ Llama-Base $>$ Dream-Base $>$ LLaDA-Base. Qwen-Base rises from $0.040$ to $0.513$, while LLaDA-Base rises from $0.143$ to $0.272$. This is the cleanest result in the thesis because parser-entry failures are almost absent and the task directly rewards finding a valid constrained arithmetic chain.

The instruct-templated condition is more model-dependent than the base condition. Dream-Instruct retains a useful pass@k profile, Llama-Instruct becomes the strongest high-$k$ model, Qwen-Instruct plateaus early, and LLaDA-Instruct has a low measured Countdown pass@128 of $0.055$. This result appears to be task- and interface-specific rather than a general failure of the checkpoint: the same model performs strongly on GSM8K, while LLaDA-Base is substantially stronger than LLaDA-Instruct on Countdown.

\begin{figure}[!htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.92\textwidth]{images/countdown_instruct_passk.pdf}
\caption{Countdown-cd4 instruct-templated pass@k. Dream-Instruct remains the strongest evaluated diffusion run, while Llama-Instruct becomes the strongest high-$k$ model. Paired bootstrap tests find Dream-Instruct $>$ Llama-Instruct at $k=1$ and Llama-Instruct $>$ Dream-Instruct at $k=128$ (Table~\ref{tab:paired-bootstrap-tests}).}
\label{fig:countdown-instruct}
\end{figure}

An earlier, smaller Countdown run was used only to inspect the effect of flat versus templated prompting before the main run was fixed. It shows that Dream-Instruct and Qwen-Instruct change substantially under flat prompting, while LLaDA-Instruct changes less. Because that diagnostic used a different generation length and evaluation set, its absolute scores are not compared with the main Countdown curves. The diagnostic table is included in Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-countdown-prompt} for traceability.
