\subsection{Countdown-cd4: budget-dependent ranking reversal}
\label{sec:results-countdown}

Countdown-cd4 gives the clearest budget-dependent ranking reversal. Under low-budget evaluation, diffusion models are competitive or leading. As $k$ increases, some AR models gain more from repeated sampling and overtake at high pass@k. Figure~\ref{fig:countdown-base} reports the base pass@k curves.

\begin{figure}[!htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.92\textwidth]{images/countdown_base_crossover.pdf}
\caption{Countdown-cd4 base pass@k crossover. Diffusion models lead at pass@1; Qwen and Llama overtake by large $k$. The crossover sits near $k=8$--$16$ in this evaluation.}
\label{fig:countdown-base}
\end{figure}

At pass@1 the ordering is LLaDA-Base $>$ Dream-Base $>$ Qwen-Base $>$ Llama-Base. At pass@128 it becomes Qwen-Base $>$ Llama-Base $>$ Dream-Base $>$ LLaDA-Base. Qwen-Base rises from $0.040$ to $0.513$, while LLaDA-Base rises from $0.143$ to $0.272$.

The instruct-templated condition is more model-dependent than the base condition. Dream-Instruct retains a useful pass@k profile, Llama-Instruct becomes the strongest high-$k$ model, Qwen-Instruct plateaus early, and LLaDA-Instruct has a low measured Countdown pass@128 of $0.055$. This result appears to be task- and interface-specific rather than a general failure of the checkpoint: the same model performs strongly on GSM8K, while LLaDA-Base is substantially stronger than LLaDA-Instruct on Countdown.

\begin{figure}[!htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.92\textwidth]{images/countdown_instruct_passk.pdf}
\caption{Countdown-cd4 instruct-templated pass@k. Dream-Instruct remains the strongest diffusion run, while Llama-Instruct becomes the strongest high-$k$ model.}
\label{fig:countdown-instruct}
\end{figure}

An earlier, smaller Countdown run was used only to inspect the effect of flat versus templated prompting before the main run was fixed. It shows that Dream-Instruct and Qwen-Instruct change substantially under flat prompting, while LLaDA-Instruct changes less. Because that diagnostic used a different generation length and evaluation set, its absolute scores are not compared with the main Countdown curves. The diagnostic table is included in Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-countdown-prompt} for traceability.
